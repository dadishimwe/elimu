# Content decisions

This log records material edits from the supplied website-copy draft, corporate profile and the user's follow-up review. The corporate profile is authoritative where the draft makes stronger claims. No unsupported project, performance or contact facts are added.

## Wording and audience pages

- “To Investors” and “To Governments” are presented as “For Investors” and “For Governments” for consistent navigation; meaning is unchanged.
- “Purpose-Built Student Accommodation” is written in sentence case. On the Investors page the first use is defined as “purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA)”.
- The tagline uses the supplied words and consistent punctuation: “We don’t just build residences; we build futures.” It appears on Home and Contact only, not as a repeated inner-page/footer signature.
- The corporate philosophy draft’s core concepts—student experience, physical and mental wellness, secure supportive environments, community, learning and leadership—are retained with light editing. The broad phrase “environmentally sustainable facilities” is replaced with the profile's narrower commitment to environmental performance, social inclusion and resilience from design through operations. No measured student outcomes are implied.
- Stronger investor-draft promises about ESG/green compliance, a de-risked asset structure, economic fundamentals, high growth, robust demographics, steady revenues, attractive risk-adjusted returns and investment outcomes are not presented as fact. The site uses the profile's investor categories, GP co-investment, milestone-based deployment, long-term inflation-linked revenue-profile wording, and governance/reporting language.
- The Investors diagram orders sourced GP/LP, milestone deployment, 25–30-year PPP/BOOT concession and long-term inflation-linked profile facts into an explicitly conceptual pathway. It is not a project cash-flow model and implies no return, capital split, guarantee or negotiated investor term. Supporting copy adds partner categories, risk allocation, lifecycle operations and governance/reporting without repeating each diagram label as its body text.
- The Government role diagram carries the government/private-partner allocation once. Adjacent prose focuses on concession term, risk allocation and the carefully bounded budget/support statements. “Without impacting your limited fiscal space” and “preserving your budgetary resources” are replaced with “minimal upfront fiscal commitment”, “limited call on public budgets”, contractually defined service expectations and any support mechanism “sized and disclosed transparently”. No no-fiscal-impact claim is made.
- “ESG-compliant”, “green compliant” and “top-tier” are not stated as achieved results. Certification wording is “designed to achieve EDGE or LEED Gold green building certification”; the site also says aligned with SDG 4 and SDG 11 and designed to meet or exceed national regulations and international standards.
- “Large-scale developments”, “affordable” as an Elimu Africa delivery promise, and claims of no fiscal impact are omitted where not supported. High-growth potential, increasing enrolment, a rapidly growing youth population, strong economic fundamentals, steady revenues and attractive returns are not invented. The source-backed shortage of safe, affordable beds relative to tertiary-enrolment growth is retained.
- Student housing is presented as core education infrastructure, with its sourced connection to skills development, research/innovation, talent retention and knowledge clusters; no benefits are quantified.
- PPP, BOOT, EPC, GP, LP, KPI and QHSE are retained only where context supports their use; this site does not invent legal/contractual definitions. “Research Triangle-style ecosystems” remains source thesis wording, not an Elimu Africa project or claimed footprint.

## Omission, privacy and visual decisions

- The deck's `[One line of relevant experience]` placeholders are omitted. The leadership page uses only the four supplied names, roles and cities; no biographies or empty placeholders are shown. Chairmanship is visually distinguished from the listed directors without extra biographical claims.
- Project names, pipelines, track record, statistics, confirmed partner/customer names, testimonials, awards, achieved certifications, stock/campus photography, AI building images and CG renders are omitted because they were not supplied or approved. Hand-authored abstract diagrams and SVG linework communicate structure without invented images.
- The schematic city rail uses Nairobi, Kigali and Johannesburg from supplied leadership/regional-presence facts. It is not a geographic map, coordinate plot, office network or distance claim. The Kigali registered office is stated separately on Contact.
- The unnamed “global facilities manager” is not named as a counterparty. Operations copy stays with sourced lifecycle-maintenance, KPI, real-time-reporting and QHSE practices.
- The deck's slide-11 email, website URL and U.S. street address are omitted. The only registered-location text is “Kigali, Rwanda”; regional presence is Nairobi, Kigali and Johannesburg. No personal phone or email is displayed. The email screenshot mentioned in the first brief was not attached.
- Source deck/document files, embedded metadata, speaker notes, internal notes and profile download are not placed in public assets. The wordmark is typographic; no absent skyline/logo artwork is traced or represented as official.
- `app.config.ts` holds required platform/project branding metadata separate from the public-site favicon and wordmark; its existing CDN URL is not presented as Elimu Africa's official logo. Replace it only with owner-approved identity artwork.
- The contact recipient and Resend mail settings are environment configuration and never appear in page copy. The application stores no enquiry in an application database. Contact success reports provider acceptance, not inbox delivery. Until provider configuration exists the endpoint reports unavailable.
- A best-effort five-attempt / 15-minute contact limiter uses a temporary keyed identifier in bounded process memory and is disclosed in the privacy notice. It is per running serverless instance, not shared edge/WAF protection. The README requires an owner-configured shared limiter before broad promotion.
- No application analytics/cookies or cookie banner are added. The project-level injected analytics are disabled and the Manus badge is hidden; that is not a claim about host/CDN technical request logs. Legal copy is generic site-use information, not jurisdiction-specific advice, and the investor notice is informational-only / not an offer or solicitation.
- No public origin was supplied. `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` remains unguessed: canonical, absolute social and sitemap URLs are emitted only when the owner configures the actual HTTPS production origin. The Vercel preview hostname is not made canonical by inference.
