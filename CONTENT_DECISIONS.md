# Content decisions

This log records the material edits from the supplied website-copy draft and corporate profile. The profile's more careful wording is authoritative where claims differ. No unsupported project, performance or contact facts have been added.

## Wording and claims

- “To Investors” and “To Governments” are presented as “For Investors” and “For Governments” for consistent page navigation; meaning is unchanged.
- “Purpose-Built Student Accommodation” is written in sentence case as “purpose-built student accommodation”. Where the abbreviation is introduced in investor copy, it is defined at first use as “purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA)”.
- The tagline uses consistent sentence-case punctuation: “We don’t just build residences; we build futures.”
- The corporate philosophy draft’s core concepts—student experience, physical and mental wellness, secure supportive environments, community, learning and leadership—are preserved with light copy-editing and sentence breaks. The draft's broad phrase “environmentally sustainable facilities” is replaced with the profile's narrower commitment to environmental performance, social inclusion and resilience from design through operations. The copy is not presented as evidence of measured student outcomes.
- The investor draft's stronger promises about ESG/green compliance, a de-risked asset structure, economic fundamentals, high growth, robust demographics, steady revenues, attractive risk-adjusted returns, financial growth and investment outcomes are not repeated. The site uses the profile's investor categories, GP co-investment, milestone-based deployment, long-term inflation-linked revenue profiles, and governance/reporting wording instead.
- The government draft's “without impacting your limited fiscal space” and “preserving your budgetary resources” are replaced with the profile's “minimal upfront fiscal commitment”, “limited call on public budgets”, “predictable, contractually defined service”, and “any support mechanism sized and disclosed transparently”.
- “ESG-compliant”, “green compliant”, and “top-tier” are not used as achieved or guaranteed results. Certification wording is “designed to achieve EDGE or LEED Gold green building certification”; the site also uses alignment with SDG 4 and SDG 11 and the source statement that each project is designed to meet or exceed national regulations and international standards.
- “Large-scale developments”, “affordable” as an Elimu Africa delivery promise, and claims that the model has no fiscal impact are omitted because the source material does not substantiate them.
- “High-growth potential”, “increasing enrolment rates”, “rapidly growing African youth”, “strong economic fundamentals” and “steady revenues/attractive returns” are omitted. The source-backed challenge is retained: tertiary enrolment is growing faster than supply of safe, affordable beds.
- The thesis is shortened to student housing as core education infrastructure and its stated connection to skills development, research excellence/innovation, talent retention and knowledge clusters. No benefits are quantified.
- O&M is expanded to “operations and maintenance”. PPP, BOOT, EPC, GP, LP, KPI and QHSE are retained only where the context makes their role clear; the site does not invent their legal/contractual definitions.
- “Research Triangle-style ecosystems” is kept as the profile's thesis wording and is not framed as a named Elimu Africa project or geographic footprint.

## Omissions and privacy

- The profile's `[One line of relevant experience]` placeholders are omitted. The leadership page contains only each supplied name, role and city, with no biographies or empty placeholders.
- Project names, pipelines, track record, statistics, partner/customer names, testimonials, awards, certification achievements, and imagery/renders are omitted because they were not supplied.
- The unnamed “global facilities manager” reference is not published as a named counterparty. The operations section retains only the source-backed practices: predictive maintenance, contractually enforced KPIs, real-time reporting, and QHSE standards.
- The profile's slide-11 email, website URL and US street address are omitted. The only registered-location text is “Kigali, Rwanda”; listed regional presence is Nairobi, Kigali and Johannesburg. No personal phone or email address is displayed.
- The email screenshot referenced by the brief was not attached. No logo was inferred, traced or recreated. A restrained text wordmark is used until approved logo files are available.
- Source deck/document files, embedded metadata, speaker notes, internal notes, and the profile download are not placed in public assets.
- The contact form has no hard-coded address. Its destination and mail-delivery configuration are environment settings; the page does not print them. Contact submissions are delivered, not stored in an application database.
- No cookie banner or third-party analytics are included. The privacy notice explains form delivery and hosting at a general level and does not claim analytics or tracking that the site does not use.
- Privacy notice and terms are generic site-use copy, not jurisdiction-specific advice. The investor notice states only that investor-facing content is informational and is not an offer or solicitation.
- The website origin was not supplied. Canonical and absolute social URLs are emitted only after `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is set to the actual HTTPS origin. The sitemap route avoids inventing a domain and includes absolute page locations once that origin is configured.
